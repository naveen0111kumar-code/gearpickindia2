Design & Ergonomics

The MX Master 3S carries forward the same iconic shape from its predecessor — a sculpted right-hand ergonomic form with a thumb rest, tiltable side scroll wheel, and a rubber-coated body that feels premium without being slippery.

At 141g it's noticeably heavier than gaming mice, but that weight works in its favour for precision-focused tasks. You won't accidentally flick it across the screen. The thumb notch cradles your hand naturally, and even after 6–8 hours of use, there's no wrist strain.

Important: This is a right-hand only mouse. Left-handed users should look at the MX Master 3S for Mac (which also comes in right-hand), or consider alternatives.

The MagSpeed Scroll Wheel — A Game Changer

If there's one feature that makes the MX Master 3S truly special, it's the electromagnetic scroll wheel. Unlike mechanical scroll wheels that click through notches, the MagSpeed wheel can scroll through 1,000 lines in a second in free-spin mode — silently.

In practice, this means you can scroll through a long codebase, a lengthy document, or a Figma file in one smooth flick. It auto-shifts between ratchet mode (precise, line-by-line) and free-spin mode depending on scroll speed. Once you've used it, going back to a normal scroll wheel feels like torture.

Silent Clicks — The Big Upgrade Over MX Master 3

The '3S' in the name stands for Silent. The primary left and right click buttons are 90% quieter than the original MX Master 3. If you work in a shared office, take calls while working, or are just someone who finds click sounds distracting, this is a big quality-of-life upgrade.

The clicks still feel tactile and responsive — Logitech hasn't sacrificed the feedback to achieve silence. This is much harder to engineer than it sounds, and they've done it well.

Performance & Multi-Device Use

The MX Master 3S connects via either Bluetooth or the included Logi Bolt USB receiver. The USB receiver gives a more stable, lower-latency connection — recommended for daily work. Bluetooth works fine for casual use.

You can pair up to 3 devices and switch between them with one button press (or a key shortcut with Logi Options+ software). If you work between a laptop, a desktop, and an iPad, this is genuinely life-changing.

The Darkfield laser sensor tracks on virtually any surface — including glass. DPI adjusts from 200 to 8,000, giving you precise control whether you're pixel-pushing in Photoshop or just browsing.

Battery Life

Logitech claims up to 70 days on a single charge. In real-world use with mixed Bluetooth and Bolt usage, expect 40–55 days. Either way, this is exceptional — you're charging it roughly once a month.

And if you do forget and the battery dies, one minute of USB-C charging gives you 3 hours of use. That's an emergency-proof mouse.

MX Master 3S vs MX Master 3 — Should You Upgrade?

The key difference is the silent click mechanism. If you already own an MX Master 3 and it's working well, there's no urgent reason to upgrade. But if you're buying new, always go for the 3S — the silence alone is worth the small price difference, and it's the current generation.

MX Master 3S vs Logitech Lift — Which to Choose?

The Logitech Lift is a vertical ergonomic mouse designed specifically for wrist pain prevention. If you have existing RSI or wrist discomfort, the Lift is the better medical choice. If you just want the best all-round productivity mouse and your wrists are fine, the MX Master 3S wins every category.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Logitech MX Master 3S good for gaming?

No. At 141g and with no high-frequency polling rate, it's not designed for gaming. For gaming, look at the Logitech G Pro X Superlight 2 or Razer Viper V3 Pro.

Does the MX Master 3S work on Mac?

Yes, perfectly. There's also a dedicated "MX Master 3S for Mac" variant in Space Grey/Pale Grey colours optimised for macOS gestures. Both versions work on Mac and Windows.

What is the warranty on MX Master 3S in India?

Logitech offers a 2-year limited hardware warranty in India when purchased from authorised sellers on Amazon.

Is there a cheaper alternative?

The Logitech MX Anywhere 3 (~₹4,500) is a compact travel mouse with many similar features. It's smaller and lacks the full ergonomic shape, but a great budget pick.
